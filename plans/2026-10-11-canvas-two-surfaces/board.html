What's real in the concept vs faked in this mockup

Real in the shipped Board: pan/zoom (this page's implementation is the real one), frames authored by Claude as plain HTML positioned via a board.json layout, pin comments stored in the existing comments.json with x/y + frame id, zoom-to-fit, and deep links (board.html#frame-b zooms to a frame — the Deck's thumbnails use this). Faked here: pins don't persist, frames are hand-placed, no Send-to-Claude wiring.

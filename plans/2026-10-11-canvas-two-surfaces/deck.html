What's real in the concept vs faked in this mockup

Real: focus/advance behavior, keyboard pick, visual option cards with thumbnails (hand-drawn divs or scaled iframe snapshots of Board frames), Board deep links, accept-remaining-leans, progress rail. Answers would flow through the existing answers.json + Send-to-Claude machinery unchanged. Faked here: persistence and the submit bar. The lean/neutral toggle is a design question on the Plan tab, not a shipped control.

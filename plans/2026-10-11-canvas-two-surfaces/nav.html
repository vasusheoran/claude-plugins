Nav v2 — slim top, actions at your thumb

The phone below is interactive — tap Pages ▾, tap Send, tap rows. Open this page on your actual phone (same URL, LAN) to feel the sizes. Proposal: on narrow screens the single top row splits into a slim title bar (top) and an action bar in the thumb zone (bottom); panels become bottom sheets. The long activity text leaves the bar entirely — it lives behind the bell, replaced by a short status chip (see the Interaction tab).

What moved where, and why:

  • Pages → a sheet. Tabs can't shrink four titles into 390px. The top bar shows only where you are; the sheet lists every page full-width with kind, comment badge, and a changed-dot.
  • Actions → bottom bar. Comment, comments panel, activity, Send sit in the thumb zone, 40px+ targets. Send keeps primary weight, always rightmost.
  • Panels → bottom sheets. Comments panel, activity feed, and the send popover all become sheets — one pattern, draggable down to dismiss.
  • Comment mode on touch: long-press an element = what C-then-click is on desktop; text selection quoting unchanged.
